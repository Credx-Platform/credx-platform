# CredX Creator Partner Program

## Positioning

Public-facing title: **CredX Creator Partner**. Internal role and database names may remain `AFFILIATE` and `SubAgent` for compatibility.

The offer is a brand partnership and commission-based residual income opportunity for creators, educators, community builders, and trusted professionals. It is not employment, a guaranteed-income offer, or permission to provide credit, legal, lending, or financial advice on behalf of CredX.

## Commission schedule

| Tier | Qualification | Initial qualifying payment | Qualifying subscription payments |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Creator | Approved starting tier | 30% | 15% for up to 12 months |
| Ambassador | 10+ paid customers in a month | 35% | 15% for up to 12 months |
| Partner | 25+ paid customers in a month | 40% | 15% for up to 12 months |
| Custom Partner | 50+ paid customers in a month | Written custom terms | Written custom terms |

- Approved recruiting override: 5% of the recruited partner's qualifying direct sales.
- Payout cadence: monthly, after a 30-day refund/chargeback hold.
- Refunded, charged-back, duplicate, fraudulent, self-referred, or unpaid transactions do not qualify and reverse related commissions.
- Rates are stored on each partner record so signed terms are not silently changed when the general program changes.

## Activation path

The measurable activation event is: **an approved creator signs the current agreement, activates the Partner Dashboard, receives a unique tracking link, reviews the disclosure rules, and publishes the first approved promotion.**

1. Creator visits `/agent` and sees the offer, tiers, safeguards, and application.
2. Application captures platform, audience band, social profile, content focus, experience, and motivation.
3. Staff reviews the application in the Creator Partner admin screen.
4. **Approve & Send** creates the tracked partner record and emails a secure, seven-day agreement link.
5. Creator signs the versioned agreement and creates a secure login.
6. Partner Dashboard shows the tracking link, activity, disclosure language, and prohibited claims.
7. Staff monitors link usage, signups, and creator compliance.

## Approved recruiting pitch

> Hey Sati — I wanted to reach out because I think you would be a strong fit for something we're building with CredX.
>
> CredX is a credit education and financial-readiness platform, and we're selecting a small group of Creator Partners to represent the brand. This is not a traditional sales job. You would receive your own Partner Dashboard, tracking link, approved content, and support from our team.
>
> When people from your audience become qualifying customers through your link, you can earn 30% of the initial qualifying payment plus 15% recurring commission on qualifying subscriptions for up to 12 months. Higher initial-payment tiers are available based on verified monthly production.
>
> There are no set hours. You can introduce CredX naturally through Instagram posts, Stories, Reels, Lives, or other channels, with clear paid-partnership disclosure. We are looking for creators who want to build a responsible long-term partnership, not just run a one-time sponsored post.
>
> If you're interested, I'd love to show you the platform, explain the agreement, and set up your tracking link.

Use this only as recruiting copy. Do not present projected earnings, guaranteed results, or the maximum tier as the applicant's starting rate.

## Compliance operating rules

- Require clear, conspicuous disclosure of the paid/commission relationship in every endorsement.
- Supply approved scripts and claims; require correction or removal of noncompliant content.
- Prohibit promises involving deletions, score changes, approvals, funding, loans, credit limits, timelines, legal representation, or guaranteed results.
- Keep sensitive consumer information in CredX. Partners must not collect or retain credit reports, SSNs, IDs, or payment information.
- Review public creator content periodically and document corrections.
- Have U.S. counsel review the agreement before broad launch, particularly contractor classification, commission payment, post-termination rights, content licensing, state law, and tax language.

## Accounting limitation

CredX currently attributes clicks and referred customer accounts. The program fields and signed rates are now recordable, but a full commission ledger, payout approvals, tax-document workflow, and automated refund/chargeback reconciliation are a separate finance implementation. Until that exists, finance must calculate and approve payouts from collected-payment records; the Partner Dashboard must not label estimates as paid or payable.
