# CredX product identity copy

These strings are the approved software-first wording for external provider setup. They are kept here so payment and social settings can be updated consistently when an authorized operator has access.

## Positioning

> CredX is a Credit Intelligence & Financial Readiness Platform that helps users understand their financial profile, organize next steps, track progress, and prepare for better financial decisions.

Software is the primary product. Education, coaching, and human review are optional secondary offerings. CredX does not guarantee score changes, deletions, approvals, funding, or other outcomes.

## Payment display copy

- **Short descriptor (22-character target):** `CREDX READINESS`
- **Masterclass product name:** `CredX Financial Readiness Masterclass`
- **Masterclass transaction description:** `CredX Financial Readiness Masterclass (digital education)`
- **Optional support line-item description:** `CredX professional review and workflow support`

The short descriptor is a proposed value for the payment provider dashboard; character limits and business-name matching rules must be confirmed in the provider account. No external payment settings were changed in this worktree.
